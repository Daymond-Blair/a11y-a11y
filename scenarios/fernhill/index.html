Frequently asked questions

Can I bring guests?

Yes. Members can bring up to two guests per day at no cost. Guests must sign in at reception.

Is parking available?

Bike storage is free. Car parking is available in the public garage next door at a discounted member rate.

Can I pause my membership?

Monthly members can pause for up to two months per year. Contact the front desk to arrange it.
